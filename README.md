# FERRULE

Files that check whether an unattended coding-agent setup can prove its own claims.

This repository carries three public surfaces:

- The purchase gate (the site root): https://haldenbuilds.github.io/ferrule/ . Paid FERRULE products are sold to buyers in the United States.
- /manifests/ : the exact file list, with sizes and checksums, for every FERRULE release, published before you buy.
- /audit/ : the free Harness Audit, complete source, MIT-licensed (the license in that folder covers that folder only). It runs read-only on your own machine and reports what your setup has, what it does not have, and what it could not see.

Every product is delivered as files, with nobody on our side: there is no support entitlement, onboarding, or service component.

Issues are open as a feedback channel: everything is read, nothing is promised.
